# Workflow

1. Register upcoming earnings companies and events.
2. Conduct pre-earnings research from approved sources.
3. Create a pre-earnings baseline with raw facts, derived metrics, interpretations, decision, scoring version, and evidence timing.
4. Lock the baseline before the earnings announcement.
5. Record the earnings announcement after publication.
6. Enter actual results, guidance updates, and key KPI outcomes.
7. Record initial market reaction and return windows.
8. Record any trade decision separately from the research hypothesis.
9. Review day 1, day 5, and day 20 outcomes.
10. Evaluate whether the pre-event hypothesis was supported, mixed, invalidated, or still open.
11. Propose score improvements without rewriting historical scores.
12. After human approval, create a new `scoring_version` and preserve the prior version.

Baseline lock means a pre-event snapshot should no longer be edited in place after the event. Corrections should be appended as a new version or as a documented correction record.
