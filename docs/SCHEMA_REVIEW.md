# Schema Review

This review checks whether the initial CSV schemas can survive realistic earnings research without forcing everything into memo fields. Recommendations are not formal schema adoption decisions yet.

| 対象テーブル | 現状の問題 | 追加候補列 | 追加すべき理由 | 追加しない場合のリスク | 代替案 | 推奨度 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `earnings_event` | Announcement timing originally used a single date/time and did not distinguish intraday, morning, after-close, or correction events. | `event_type`, `announcement_session`, `return_base_price_policy`; later candidates: `release_document_type`, `briefing_datetime`, `revision_only_flag` | Covers intraday earnings, after-close releases, morning releases, guidance revisions, monthly releases, and briefing-driven reactions. | Returns may be measured from the wrong price; guidance revision-only events may be mixed with ordinary earnings. | Adopted first three fields; defer document-level fields to evidence. | high |
| `earnings_event` | Fiscal standards were not captured. | `accounting_standard`; later candidate: `consolidation_scope` | IFRS, Japanese GAAP, and US GAAP can change profit comparability and KPI meaning. | Operating income and net income comparisons may be misleading. | Adopted event-level standard. | high |
| `pre_earnings_baseline` | Consensus/guidance fields omit ordinary income and net income. | `market_consensus_ordinary_income`, `market_consensus_net_income`, `company_guidance_ordinary_income`, `company_guidance_net_income` | Japanese earnings often discuss operating income, ordinary income, net income, and EPS differently by sector. | Analysts may force key values into reason text, weakening validation. | Add a flexible metric table after SQLite decision. | high |
| `pre_earnings_baseline` | Segment and industry KPI expectations are summarized as scores only. | `kpi_observation` table; later candidates: `segment_expectation_summary` | SaaS ARR/NRR, retail same-store sales, manufacturing backlog, construction orders, restaurant traffic, finance AUM, real estate occupancy, and trading company resource sensitivity differ by industry. | Research loses the concrete driver behind the score. | Adopt generic KPI table linked to evidence. | high |
| `post_earnings_review` | Actual result fields omit ordinary income, net income, and segment/KPI actuals. | `actual_ordinary_income`, `actual_net_income`; KPI actuals now go to `kpi_observation` | Needed to compare the exact metric that mattered before the event. | Post-event review becomes narrative-only and harder to score later. | Adopt generic KPI table; defer profit-line expansion. | high |
| `post_earnings_review` | Return windows originally did not state the actual reference price. | `return_reference_price_type`, `return_reference_price`, `return_reference_price_datetime` | Prior close, next open, announcement-time price, and VWAP produce different conclusions. | Day0/day1 return comparisons become inconsistent. | Adopted review-side reference fields; price-data source remains open. | high |
| `company_master` | Accounting standard and country are missing. | `country`, `accounting_standard_default` | Cross-market and IFRS/GAAP interpretation differ. | Company-level assumptions are duplicated in event notes. | Add event-level standard first if standards can change. | medium |
| `tso_snapshot` | Current mapping is provisional and compresses TSO values. | `tso_log_schema_version`, `source_column_set_hash` | Helps prove which TSO_LOG contract produced the snapshot. | Later TSO_LOG changes may silently change meaning. | Keep source file/hash and mapping draft until formal 28 columns are approved. | high |
| `hypothesis_log` | Hypotheses do not explicitly distinguish raw fact, derived metric, interpretation, decision, and outcome. | `claim_category`, `evidence_ids` | Reinforces the system principle that facts and interpretations are separate. | AI interpretation could be mistaken for verified evidence. | Use `evidence` and parent links until schema is stabilized. | medium |
| `score_definition` | Score version rows do not identify approval status. | `approval_status`, `approved_by`, `approved_at` | Prevents provisional components from being treated as production scoring. | Experimental score versions may look official. | Record in `DECISIONS.md` for now. | medium |
| `evidence` | New lineage table is polymorphic and does not enforce every related ID through static foreign keys. | `relation_status`, `lineage_group_id` | Polymorphic evidence can point to several entity types; a lineage group can tie briefing, release, and reaction evidence together. | Cross-reference mistakes may slip through if custom validation is weak. | Custom validator checks known entity IDs now; SQLite can enforce separate link tables later. | medium |

## Immediate Recommendation

`event_type`, `announcement_session`, `accounting_standard`, `return_base_price_policy`, review-side return reference fields, and append-only `kpi_observation` rows are adopted for sample/schema validation. Additional profit-line fields, document-level release taxonomy, and specialized KPI tables remain candidates rather than formal schema.
